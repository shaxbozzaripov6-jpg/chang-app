2. Anonim simptomlar statistikasi · Anonymised symptoms

/api/research/symptoms.csv

Foydalanuvchilar ixtiyoriy va rozilik bilan yuritgan sog'liq kundaligidan tuzilgan. Shaxsni aniqlash mumkin bo'lmasligi uchun faqat kamida 5 kishi yozuv qoldirgan shahar-kunlar chiqariladi. Ism, Telegram ID, aniq joylashuv yoki izohlar hech qachon berilmaydi.

Built from voluntary, consented symptom diaries. Only city-days with at least 5 distinct users are published (k-anonymity). No names, IDs, exact locations or notes are ever shared.

Ustunlar: city, date, users, severity_mean (0–3), har bir simptom soni (cough, breath, wheeze, eyes, nose, throat, headache, fatigue, skin, fever, none), pm10_mean, pm25_mean, dust_max.
